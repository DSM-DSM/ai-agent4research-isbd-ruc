AI Agent 技能AI Agent Skills

AI Agent 就是能替你动手干活的 AI 助手；下面每一项「技能」，是教会它的一种新本事。技能皆可一键安装，两项附能直接打开的真实成品。An AI agent is an assistant that does hands-on work for you; each skill below teaches it a new trick. One-line install; two ship demos you can open right now.

论文精读引擎Paper Reading Enginepaper-explainer

01

刚进一个陌生领域，论文 PDF 读不懂也读不完？把 PDF 都丢给它——每篇变成一篇像老师带着读的中文讲解网页，读完全部还会画出一张知识点关系图。New to a field, staring at a pile of PDFs? Drop them all in — each becomes an annotated page that walks you through the paper, and a knowledge graph ties everything together at the end.

举个例子For example你刚接触「孟德尔随机化」，手头有 10 篇核心论文。交给它之后，先看阅读路径知道该先读哪篇；每篇的重点公式都有大白话解释；最后一张知识图谱告诉你哪几篇的方法是相通的——一周的入门功课，两三天过完。You're starting on Mendelian randomization with ten core papers. It hands you a reading path, explains every key formula in plain words, and maps which papers share methods — a week of onboarding done in two or three days.

输入Input
一个装着论文 PDF 的文件夹a folder of paper PDFs
输出Output
一套互链的精读网页系统：每篇一份详解，外加阅读路径、背景词典和知识图谱a linked reading site: one annotated page per paper, plus reading path, glossary and knowledge graph

网页设计引擎Web Design Enginehtml-beautify

02

不会写网页，又想要一个不套模板、拿得出手的学术页面？把需求说给它，它自己找设计参考、直接产出能用的网页——你现在看到的这个网站就是它做的。Want a polished academic page without writing code? Tell it what you need; it picks a design direction and ships a working page — this very site is its work.

举个例子For example组会前想给老师看一页「实验进展看板」，自己又不会前端。跟它说清楚要展示什么，它先挑一个合适的设计风格，再生成带图表、能换主题的页面——半天之内就能把链接发出去。Before a lab meeting you want a progress dashboard for your advisor, but you don't do frontend. Describe what to show, and it returns a themed page with charts — a shareable link within half a day.

输入Input
想要的风格和展示方式，用大白话说就行the style and layout you want, in plain words
输出Output
一个能直接打开使用的网页a working page, ready to open

提示词优化Prompt Enhancerprompt-enhancer

03

跟 AI 说话总说不到点子上、它答非所问？用大白话把你想干嘛告诉它，它帮你翻译成 AI 能听懂、条理清楚的要求，还会告诉你改了哪里——用久了，你自然就学会怎么向 AI 提要求。AI keeps misunderstanding what you asked? Say it in plain words — this skill rewrites your request into one the AI can act on, and tells you what it changed. Use it long enough and you learn to brief AI yourself.

举个例子For example你只丢一句「帮我改改这段代码」，AI 只能瞎猜你想要什么。它会把这句话扩成「目标是什么、输入输出是什么、有什么限制」，AI 拿到就能一次改对。"Fix this code" tells the AI nothing about the goal. It expands that into target, inputs, outputs and constraints — and the fix lands in one shot.

输入Input
一句模糊的想法a vague one-line idea
输出Output
一份拆好步骤、拿来就能用的提示词 markdowna step-by-step markdown prompt, ready to run

会话接力棒Session Batonbaton

04

AI 的聊天窗口有「记忆上限」：聊久了它会忘掉前面说过的话，换个窗口更是全部重来。这个技能让 AI 收工前把「做到哪了、踩过什么坑、下一步干嘛」写成一页备忘，下次新开窗口把备忘交给它，接着干。AI chat windows forget: past the context limit, earlier talk is gone — and a new window starts from zero. This skill has the AI write down "where we are, what failed, what's next" into a one-page baton the next session picks up.

举个例子For example改论文的任务聊到一半窗口满了，存一页接力棒。第二天新开对话，AI 读完接力棒直接从上次断的地方继续，不用你从头解释一遍论文背景和已经试过的改法。A paper-editing task hits the context limit mid-way — drop a baton. Next day, a fresh session reads it and continues exactly where you left off, no re-explaining the background or the fixes you already tried.

输入Input
聊到一半的会话上下文the in-progress chat
输出Output
一页 markdown 接力棒，交给下一个会话接着干a one-page markdown baton for the next session
来源Origin
整合上游项目，按科研工作流深度定制（落棒前 git 核实的铁律等）Upstream project, deeply customized for research workflows (git-verify-before-drop iron rule, etc.)

实时搜索引擎AnySearchanysearch

05

AI 懂的东西像一本几年前印好的百科全书，书出版之后的事它一页都没有。这个技能等于把一个搜索框放到它手边：网页、新闻当场查，股票、学术论文这些专门渠道也搜得到，几个问题还能一起查。An AI's knowledge is an encyclopedia printed years ago — nothing after the print date is in there. This skill puts a search box at its side: web and news on the spot, stock and academic channels too, several queries at once.

举个例子For example你问 AI「某个模型上周更新了什么」，它答不上来——书里没这一页。装上它再问一遍，它当场去搜新闻和官网，把更新日期、更新内容、原文链接一起端回来。Ask an AI what a model shipped last week and it draws a blank — that page isn't in its book. With this skill it searches news and release notes on the spot, and returns the date, the changes and the source link.

社媒搜索引擎Social Media Searchagent-reach

06

百度、谷歌搜不到小红书、微博、B 站里的帖子——这些内容锁在 App 里，搜索引擎进不去。这个技能相当于把你平时刷手机的账号借给 AI：它替你走进这些 App，把帖子和评论翻出来读给你。Posts on Xiaohongshu, Weibo and Bilibili never show up in regular search — they live inside the apps. This skill lends the AI your logged-in accounts: it walks into those apps for you and reads out the posts and comments.

举个例子For example想知道「读研到底苦不苦」，用百度只能搜到官方介绍。它替你翻小红书上研究生们发的吐槽帖，把几十条评论里反复出现的压力、宿舍、导师话题整理成一段总结——这些话只有 App 里才有。Wondering what grad school is really like? Regular search returns official blurbs. This skill browses Xiaohongshu posts by grad students and boils dozens of comments down to the recurring themes — stress, dorms, advisors — words that only exist inside the app.
