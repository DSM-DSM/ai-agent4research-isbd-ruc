×××，中国人民大学统计与大数据研究院副教授、博士生导师。主要研究兴趣为高维统计推断与统计机器学习，近年的工作集中在稀疏估计的极小极大最优性、异方差噪声下的协方差估计，以及基于干预数据的因果发现。研究方法上注重理论保证与算法实现的结合，多篇工作发表于 Journal of the American Statistical Association、Annals of Statistics 等期刊与 NeurIPS、ICML 等会议。××× is an Associate Professor at the School of Statistics and Big Data, Renmin University of China, and a doctoral supervisor. His research centers on high-dimensional statistical inference and statistical machine learning, with recent work on minimax optimality of sparse estimation, covariance estimation under heteroskedastic noise, and causal discovery from interventional data. He publishes in journals such as the Journal of the American Statistical Association and Annals of Statistics, and conferences including NeurIPS and ICML.

×××，中国人民大学数学科学研究院副教授、博士生导师。主要研究兴趣为解析数论与调和分析，近年的工作集中在 L 函数零点分布与黎曼 ζ 函数的均值估计，方法上兼重解析工具与组合技术。多篇工作发表于 Journal of Number Theory、Mathematische Annalen 等期刊。××× is an Associate Professor at the Institute for Mathematical Sciences, Renmin University of China.
